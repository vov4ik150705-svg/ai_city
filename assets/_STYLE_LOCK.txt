Corporator v2 — STYLE LOCK (read before every GenerateImage)

WHAT "STYLE" MEANS (and what it does NOT mean)
  Same WORLD, not the same BUILDING.
  Lighting + camera + finish are shared. Colors, silhouette, materials, roof,
  and massing MUST be unique per object. Do NOT clone City Hall (no cream
  porticos, no cobalt tiled hip roof, no cupola, no matching masonry box).
  Do NOT paint every sprite in Hall's cream + cobalt.
  Previous-version PNGs are IDENTITY only (what the object is). They are
  boring/pixel — do not copy them 1:1; redesign them as unique painterly
  3D miniatures that still read as the same object.

CAMERA (shared)
  2:1 isometric, ~30° from above, NO vanishing-point perspective.
  Object centered, occupies >= 2/3 of the frame.

LIGHT (shared — this is the only Hall lesson)
  Sun UPPER LEFT.
  Bright tops and left faces. Darker right faces.
  Soft AO in corners and under eaves. No rim-light from the right. No night.

FINISH (shared)
  Painterly 3D miniature as RAW identity only. Game sprite is the pixelize output:
  grid-aligned, 53-colour lock, 1px outline. Do not ship unquantized v2 into the atlas.

BACKGROUND (mandatory)
  Solid flat MAGENTA #FF00FF on every non-object pixel.
  NO ground plane, NO grass under the object, NO dirt cube, NO drop-shadow
  blob on the canvas (shadows are a later layer). NO LV badge. NO HUD.

UNIQUENESS (mandatory — fail the sprite if it looks like Hall or a sibling)
  Different footprint/silhouette from every other v2 already on disk.
  Different roof language (hip / sawtooth / flat+HVAC / dome / glass cube /
  shed / campus bar — pick ONE that fits THIS object, not Hall's).
  Different wall material (brick / glass / steel panel / clapboard / stone
  rustication / tile / concrete — pick what the function needs).
  Accent color from the object's job or district, NOT from Hall:
    compute/NVDA  — green glow, dark tech
    energy/TSLA   — crimson, industrial
    logistics/AMZN — ochre/amber warehouse
    consumer/AAPL — pale glass, highlight, warm retail
    automation/MSFT — navy campus, long and low
    finance/COIN  — deep blue, vault/tower language
    base workshop — warm brick + steel, production
    base hub      — civic/training, not a hall clone
    base power    — small electrical, insulators
    base bank     — vault, not a mini City Hall
  Windows: at least two horizontal rows on buildings; amber and/or cool glass.
  Beacons: huge corp mark on a facade (>= 1/3 of that wall) + roof ticker.
  Product: Corporator, $CORP. Sixth stock COIN. NEVER Google / GOOGL / G.
