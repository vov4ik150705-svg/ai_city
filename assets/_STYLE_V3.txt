WHAT "STYLE" MEANS
  Same WORLD, not the same BUILDING.
  Shared language: chunky toy volume, neon in windows, readable silhouette from orbit.
  NOT photoreal. NOT pixelize. NOT a clone of the NVIDIA research brick on every house.

CAMERA (shared)
  High orbit around the island. Limited tilt. No first-person fly. No vanishing-point street.
  Object reads as a diorama piece on the XZ lot, not a 2D rhombus sticker.

LIGHT (shared)
  Night and neon are the native phase. Day keeps the city on but kills signage.
  Remove baked sun from albedo (Meshy: Remove Lighting ON).
  Windows MUST glow via a separate emission map. Do not paint sun onto walls.

FINISH (shared language, unique material)
  One language: toy scale, hard edges, neon slits.
  Different material per building: granite, enamel, glass, steel, tile, concrete —
  pick what the FUNCTION needs. Do not paint every hero as dark NVIDIA brick + green eye.
  NVIDIA dark brick + green eye is legal ONLY on the AI Research Center.

UNIQUENESS (mandatory — fail the asset if it looks like a sibling)
  Different footprint/silhouette from every other hero already on disk.
  Different roof language.
  Accent from the job or district, NOT from one hero:
