WORLD
  The map is the immutable authored Island from the 2026-09-06 snapshot.
  Do not cut, remesh, recolor, resize, or regenerate it.
  Add vertical city volume with separate hardscape decks, retaining walls,
  stairs, pads, and plazas.
  No grass roof. No water. No units. No pack-grid geometry.

TARGET
  Stylized low-poly cyber diorama.
  Bright blue day is the primary frame.
  Dusk and night strengthen signs, windows, and lamps.
  The frame must read as one city on a rock, not GLBs on a field.

COMPOSITION
  City Hall is the civic dominant.
  Use four to six asymmetric building groups.
  Do not place all buildings in an even ring or aim every facade at origin.
  Use the full useful top with deliberate plazas, garden pockets, and overlooks.
  Approve the gray blockout before materials or decor.

HEIGHT
  At least three readable hardscape levels.
  L0 is exposed Island, never a separate deck or a cut passage.
  Three levels: two 160–240 m gaps, total 320–480 m.
  Optional L3: three 100–200 m gaps, total 360–520 m.
  Final values require owner approval at LOOK-04.

GROUND
  Measured categories sum to 100% of the 7200×7200 useful top.
  Target: 68% hardscape, 28% exposed canonical rock, 4% planter soil.
  Grass coverage is 0%.
  Checker paving is local to a civic plaza, never the whole city.

BUILDINGS
  The 18 files in models/*.glb are immutable sources.
  Runtime derivatives use local roots at the footprint base.
  A building GLB owns its signs, windows, and building-specific roof props.
  Preserve distinct material languages by function; never repaint all assets
  as dark NVIDIA brick.

MATERIALS
  Base surfaces remain readable with emissive disabled.
  Remove baked lighting from albedo.
  BaseColor is sRGB; data maps are linear.
  Do not strip normal/AO/ORM maps before a visual A/B comparison.
  Runtime texture side is 1024 unless an owner-approved measured exception exists.

EMISSIVE
  Separate categories: sign, window, lamp, sky, cloud, buildingSurface.
  Runtime strength = exported strength multiplied by phase gain.
  Bright day keeps signs at a visible baseline; windows are faint; lamps are off.
  Sky and clouds are unlit and never phase-glow or bloom.

DECOR
  Start from the root decoration/ library and run license/style/scale/budget intake.
  Use authored groups and GPU instances.
  Trees, lamps, short rails, benches, planters, bollards, and small roof props are legal.
  People, cars, drones, robots, mobs, water, alpha-card forests, and random scatter are not.

CLOUDS
  Four purpose-built closed chunky silhouettes.
  Do not reuse tree fragments as clouds.
  Use near/mid/far depth bands and at least two clouds below the island edge.
  No billboard, particle, CSS glyph, or volumetric system.

RENDER
  Viewer and client use one loader, anchor path, camera math, material policy,
  light rig, and post stack.
  sRGB + ACES Filmic + OutputPass.
  One shadow-casting key, restrained fill, selective bloom on emissive only.
  A Blender viewport is not a production visual proof.

CAMERA
  Fit from scene bounds and the safe frame; do not hardcode 2200 m.
  Initial lookdev: FOV 32–40°, azimuth 35–50°, elevation 30–40° above horizon.
  The island fills 72–86% of the frame width while the cliff and spire remain visible.

ACCEPTANCE
  Every LOOK provides runtime captures and waits for owner GO.
  A green test, a node count, or a draw-call formula does not accept the picture.
  Only ACCEPT LOOK-11 unlocks HUD work.
